## Traceability Notes

- **Phase 1A verification:**
  - `apps/api/tests/integration/students-scoping.test.ts` verifies that `admin` sees all 60 students, `teacher1` sees exactly 20 students across assigned sections 6A and 7A, `teacher2` sees exactly 10 students in section 6A, `guardian1` sees exactly 2 linked children, and `guardian2` sees exactly 1 linked child.
  - Cross-family access (for example `guardian1` requesting `GET /api/v1/students/:child3Id`) returns `404 Not Found` without leaking existence.
  - Immutability of append-only records (`AuditLog` and `LedgerEntry`) is enforced by Mongoose pre-hooks and repository design, and tested in `apps/api/tests/integration/audit.test.ts`.
  - Database integrity is enforced with `$jsonSchema` validators, unique and partial-unique indexes (created by `migrate-mongo` migrations and checked in `schema-validators.test.ts`). MongoDB has no foreign keys, so references between collections are validated in the service layer.
- **A1 and A2 (attendance):** API and UI delivered in Phases 2A and 2B (teacher edit window, idempotent bulk upsert, 50-concurrent-request test).
- **A3 (exams and grading):** Pure-function integer grading, ranking, snapshotting and report card API delivered in Phase 3A; exam and report card screens follow in Phase 3B.
- **A4 (guardian scoping):** Scoping is already enforced for students, attendance and report cards. The full guardian portal and invoice scoping are finalized in Phases 4 and 5.
- **A5 (fees and ledger reconciliation):** Invoicing, payment idempotency and automated ledger reconciliation are delivered in Phase 4.
